About Us

History
Community Food Bank was founded in 2015 after local community leaders saw a rising need for food support. What began as a small weekly distribution has grown into a network of volunteers, donors, and partner organisations providing regular services across multiple neighbourhoods.

Mission & Vision
Our mission is to reduce hunger and improve nutrition stability through direct food support and education. Our vision is a community where every household has reliable access to nutritious food.

Our Team
We are volunteer-run with a small paid coordinator team. Volunteers are trained in safe food handling and beneficiary dignity practices.

Values

  • Compassion — We prioritise dignity for everyone we serve.
  • Community — We partner with local stakeholders and donors.
  • Transparency — We report our activities and use of donations publicly.

Quick Facts

Founded: 2015

Regions served: Johannesburg (multiple suburbs)

Volunteers: 120+ active volunteers
