Our Services

We provide a range of services to support individuals and families facing food insecurity. Below is a summary of our main programs and how to access them.

Weekly Food Parcels

Households can register for a weekly parcel containing staple foods, fresh produce when available, and hygiene items.

Soup Kitchen

Hot meals served daily on-site. Meals are prepared by volunteers and local partner kitchens.

Nutrition Workshops

Workshops on affordable healthy eating, meal planning, and basic food preservation.

Mobile Outreach

We run pop-up distributions in hard-to-reach areas. Dates announced on our social pages.

Partner Referral

We partner with health and social services to refer families for extra support where needed.

Donations & Partnerships

We accept in-kind donations and work with businesses for food rescue and sponsorships.

How to access services

  1. Visit the Enquiry page to register a request (or call us).
  2. Provide basic household information — we assess and schedule collection/delivery.
  3. For emergencies, call our phone number during working hours.
