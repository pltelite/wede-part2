What we do

Community Food Bank is a volunteer-run non-profit committed to alleviating food insecurity. We distribute food parcels weekly, run a soup kitchen, and host nutrition education programs for families.

Our core services

Food Parcels

Nutritious parcels for families, distributed weekly with fresh and non-perishable items.

Soup Kitchen

Daily hot meals served on-site to people in need and those experiencing homelessness.

Community Outreach

Mobile outreach and pop-up distributions in underserved neighbourhoods.

How you can help

  • Volunteer your time — pack parcels, assist at the soup kitchen.
  • Donate non-perishable goods or funds.
  • Share our mission with your network to raise awareness.
